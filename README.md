## Project Overview

The Offgrid Clothing website is a professional e‑commerce platform designed for a small clothing business based in Malanulele, Limpopo. The website aims to establish a strong online presence, enable online shopping, and improve communication between the business and its customers.

### Business Background

Offgrid was founded in 2022 by Thabo, a young entrepreneur with a vision to make quality fashion accessible to everyone. The brand specialises in stylish, affordable clothing for diverse customer segments and has grown through word‑of‑mouth and community engagement.

---

## Website Goals and Objectives

### Primary Goals

1. **Increase Online Presence** – Establish a professional digital footprint.
2. **Enable Online Shopping** – Allow customers to browse and purchase products online.
3. **Improve Communication** – Provide multiple channels for customer interaction.
4. **Build Brand Credibility** – Create a professional image that builds trust.
5. **Expand Market Reach** – Attract customers beyond the local area.

### Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

| KPI | Target |
|-----|--------|
| Website Traffic | 500+ unique visitors/month |
| Conversion Rate | 5% in first quarter |
| Contact Form Submissions | 20+ enquiries/month |
| Bounce Rate | Below 40% |
